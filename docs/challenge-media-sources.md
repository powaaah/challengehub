## New challenge

The 50 pull-ups / 100 push-ups challenge is described by athlete Malin Malle:
https://www.malinmalle.com/blog/i-tried-calisthenics-endurance-for-the-first-time

ChallengeHub uses a fixed order (all pull-ups before push-ups), a continuous timer and a 300-second target. The exact execution rules are stated on the challenge page. Times are self-reported. No external record claim or Melvinbrat video is included; Stefan explicitly said the video is not needed.
